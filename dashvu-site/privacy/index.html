5. Cookies, Analytics, and Tracking Technologies

Vedinci Tech LLC is committed to transparency about how information is collected and used when visitors interact with DashVu and its email communications.

A. DashVu Website

The DashVu website is operated by Vedinci Tech LLC and hosted by Netlify.

Our current website does not include Google Analytics, Meta Pixel, TikTok Pixel, Google Ads, or other identified client-side advertising tracking technologies.

Netlify Web Analytics is disabled.

Our technical review did not identify browser cookies or local browser storage being created by the DashVu website during the interactions tested.

Netlify may process technical information, including IP addresses, browser information, and website requests, to deliver, secure, and maintain our website.

B. Omnisend-Hosted Signup Page

When you select "Join Waitlist," you are directed to a signup page hosted by Omnisend, our email marketing provider.

Omnisend loads scripts and supporting resources to operate the signup page and monitor technical errors.

When the signup page opens, Omnisend records a form-view event. This event includes information such as the date and time, page address and title, form identifier, and whether the visitor uses a mobile device.

Our initial technical review did not identify browser-accessible cookies or local browser storage before email submission.

Omnisend may process additional technical information or use cookies and similar identifiers during signup and subsequent interactions. The exact technologies involved depend on the services and settings in use.

C. Email-Open Tracking and Consent

DashVu uses Omnisend to manage email subscriptions and distribute waitlist, product, and marketing communications.

We use a double opt-in process requiring subscribers to confirm their email address before completing their subscription.

We also provide a separate, optional choice concerning email-open tracking.

With the consent-based email-open tracking setting enabled, Omnisend includes an invisible tracking image in marketing emails only for subscribers whose email-open tracking consent is recorded as given.

Subscribers who decline tracking or have not recorded a tracking preference do not receive email-open tracking pixels in newly sent marketing emails.

Email-open tracking helps us understand email engagement and improve our communications. A recorded email open does not necessarily mean a recipient personally read the message.

Subscribers may update or withdraw their email-open tracking consent through our email preference management options without cancelling their marketing subscription.

Withdrawing consent applies to future emails and does not automatically delete historical tracking information.

Omnisend may separately process information about interactions with links in our emails. Email-open tracking consent controls do not govern all other forms of email interaction tracking.

D. Advertising and Behavioral Tracking

Vedinci Tech does not currently sell personal information or share it for cross-context behavioral advertising.

Our current website and waitlist signup page do not include identified Meta Pixel, TikTok Pixel, Google Ads, or comparable advertising pixels.

E. Your Privacy Choices

You may control certain cookies and browser storage through your browser settings.

You may choose whether to allow email-open tracking when subscribing to DashVu communications and may update that preference through the available subscriber preference controls.

You may unsubscribe from DashVu marketing emails at any time using the unsubscribe mechanism provided in our emails.

For privacy-related questions or requests, contact privacy@vedinci.com.

If we introduce additional analytics, advertising, or tracking technologies in the future, we will update this policy and provide applicable choices or obtain consent where required by law.
